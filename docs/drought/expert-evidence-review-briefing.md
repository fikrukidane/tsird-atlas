# Expert Evidence Review — participant briefing and invitation

**Use:** protected Tigray Insights Expert Evidence Review page and individual
reviewer invitation.
**Status:** draft participant-facing copy, 2026-09-16.

## Page briefing

### Why we are asking for this review

TSIRD is an experimental Tigray-focused evidence platform. The Drought
Intelligence work brings together retained rainfall, vegetation and
agricultural-water-use observations, alongside clearly separated FEWS NET
provider context. It is intended to support learning and discussion; it is
not an official forecast, food-security classification, response-priority
decision, or allocation tool.

Numbers and satellite observations can show a pattern, but they cannot by
themselves explain local conditions. Irrigation, market access, conflict or
displacement, crop timing, livestock conditions, local water availability, and
data gaps can change what a pattern means in a particular Tabia. Your review
helps us test whether the displayed evidence is plausible, what important
context is missing, and where the approach should be improved before it is
treated as more than a research prototype.

### What you will do

You will review sixteen deliberately varied Tabia cases. For each case, you
will see the retained evidence and its limitations. Please record, in your own
professional judgment:

1. whether the evidence appears plausible for the place and time shown;
2. any local condition or confounder that could change its interpretation;
3. evidence that is missing, uncertain, or potentially misleading; and
4. a short explanation or follow-up question.

There is no expected answer. A disagreement, uncertainty, or recommendation
not to interpret a case is useful feedback.

### What this review will not do

Submitting a review does **not** validate the model, change a map category,
create an operational recommendation, publish an official classification, or
commit any organisation to an action. Responses are reviewed as structured
input for a later, documented calibration discussion. Any future change will
require a separate decision, versioned testing, and clear public explanation.

### Privacy and participation

The review is for invited participants. Other reviewers cannot see your
response. Please avoid including personal, sensitive, or operationally risky
information that is not necessary for the review. You may save a draft and
return later. Contact the review administrator if you need a correction or
withdrawal considered under the stated response-retention policy.

### Before you begin

Use the individual account and password-setup link provided in your invitation.
Review the evidence labels and dates carefully: displayed records are retained
observations and provider context, not current conditions unless the page says
so. Please state uncertainty where it exists.
